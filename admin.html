Orders

Every delivery and self-collection order a customer submits lands here, with the WhatsApp message sent alongside it. Changing status here is immediate — it doesn't need Publish. Delivery zones, collection points, and delivery time slots are all configured on the Settings tab.

Customers

Everyone who has submitted the site's entry-gate form or placed an order, deduplicated by phone number. This list only grows once you turn on "Require visitor details before browsing" on the Settings tab, or once orders start coming in.

Delivery zones & pricing

Priced by area at checkout — this is the delivery charge customers see and pay.

Columns: Area, Emirate, Charge, Free Above (optional), Available (optional — "no" to hide a zone). A .csv always works; a .xlsx needs an internet connection to load the Excel reader.

Self-collection points

Shown to customers who choose to collect their order themselves instead of having it delivered.

Delivery time slots

The windows customers can choose from when scheduling a delivery — e.g. "9am – 12pm". These are requested windows, not a guaranteed courier booking, so keep them broad enough to actually meet.

Customer capture

Require every visitor to share their name and phone number before they can browse the site — useful for building a customer list, but note that some casual visitors will leave rather than fill in a form first. Off by default, and easy to switch off again.
